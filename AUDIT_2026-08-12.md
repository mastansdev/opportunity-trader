## 4 · THE MEASUREMENT BUG THAT BLOCKS EVERYTHING

Every number above applies the 2.5% stop to a **close-to-close** move. That
cannot see a stock that dipped through the stop, took you out, then recovered.

**So every figure here is optimistic and the ordering may be wrong.**

`from_chip_time()` already computes `worst` from minute bars. Until the stop is
applied against that instead, no rule above should be changed on these numbers.

**This is the one thing to fix first.**
