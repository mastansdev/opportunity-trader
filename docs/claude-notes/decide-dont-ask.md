simple over complex, only real opportunities, never average across
stocks, ON=real/OFF=paper. Asking him to re-decide those wastes the
time he is paying me to save, and it reads as not having listened.

**How to apply:** the shape he asked for, 4 Sep 2026 — *"but explain
me first whats the issue, whats your plan to solve that & move on with
solving code"*. One message: what is broken, how it will be fixed, then
go and fix it. No question mark at the end, no waiting. Keep stopping for
things that are genuinely irreversible, that risk real money, or where
his rules genuinely do not decide it — and when stopping, say which of
those three it is. Present measurements and a recommendation, not a
menu.
