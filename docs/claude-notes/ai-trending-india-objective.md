His own framing of AI Trending India, stated 22 Aug 2026:

> "its separate system that works & earn money 24/7 no matter how
> small/big that amount"

**Why this matters for how it gets built:** the goal is *continuous
autonomous earning*, not scale. A system making ₹100/day unattended beats
one making ₹1,000/day that needs him at the keyboard. He already runs
Opportunity Trader, which demands his attention during market hours — this
one is explicitly meant to be the opposite: it should keep working while he
is doing something else, including overnight and on market holidays.

**How to apply:** when trading off between reach and autonomy, favour
autonomy. Prefer things that run unattended and fail safely over things
that need a human in the loop — while still honouring the instruction's
Phase 10 rule that sensitive topics (politics, medical, legal, financial
advice, allegations) stay human-reviewed. Reliability, error recovery and
observability are features here, not polish. Milestone 12 (₹100/day) is a
real milestone, not a warm-up.
